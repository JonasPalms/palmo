## About

A native desktop app for tracking time - fast, minimal, and local-first.

## Why I Built It

I wanted a simple timer I could trust to stay local and feel instant. Most time trackers feel heavier than they need to be - this one stays out of the way.

## Features

- **Start/stop timer** with real-time display
- **Session history** to review what you've worked on
- **Local SQLite database** - your data stays on your machine
- **Dark/light mode** with a clean, minimal UI
- **Favorites** for quick time entry on recurring tasks
- **Keyboard shortcuts** for power users

## Tech Stack

Built with **Tauri** (Rust) + **Svelte**, with data stored locally in **SQLite**.

## How It's Put Together

Tauri handles the native layer, Svelte renders the UI, and SQLite stores sessions and favorites locally.
